The Book

Nobody Is Coming
To Make You Rich

Investing, trading and AI: what your platform, your advisor and your algorithm aren’t telling you.

Kindle on Amazon → Amazon UK
No affiliate links, here or in the book.

Every charge you pay is disclosed somewhere. Almost none of it is disclosed where you’d look.

The spread you cross on the way in. The financing that accrues overnight. The currency leg you did without noticing. The custody arrangement you never read.

This book is written by someone who trades physical commodities for a living, and builds systematic trading models in their own time. It explains, mechanically and without selling you anything, how the machinery actually works.

Five parts. Twenty-one chapters.

Part One

What it’s costing you

The spread, financing, currency, fees, and who is on the other side of your trade.

Part Two

Setting yourself up

Choosing a platform, opening and funding an account, your first trade, and where your money is actually held.

Part Three

What you’re actually buying

Wrappers, domicile, leverage, and the one asset class that isn’t a company.

Part Four

How to judge a claim

Track records, backtests, the four numbers that stop you being fooled, and following the incentive.

Part Five

AI, honestly

What it does well, what it doesn’t, how to build something without fooling yourself, and what to ask before you pay for any of it.

From chapter one

Open any platform and look at a price. You will probably see one number, large and clean, perhaps with a green or red arrow beside it. That number does not exist.

Figure 1.1. The price that doesn’t exist. One of twenty-nine figures in the book.

What this is not

This is not financial advice, and the author is not a licensed adviser. There are no tips, no signals, no projections and no returns claimed. Platforms are compared on their own published charges and documents, quoted and dated, and no platform is ranked. There are no affiliate links. What it gives you is the mechanism, so that the decision is an informed one.
